# Reader Experience Technique Bank V1

## Purpose

This bank preserves validated reader-experience craft that was previously extracted from source books but was often classified as Supporting and therefore failed to reach production/final acceptance. It is deliberately separate from Novel DNA.

It may guide scene design, diagnosis and final reader gates. It may not authorize plot copying or style imitation.

## Governance

- `Novel DNA != Reader Experience Technique`.
- A technique does not need to be book-unique to be production-critical.
- Supporting status is not a reason to discard a technique from writing or validation.
- Use only abstract mechanisms; never copy source scenes, wording, characters or recognizable style.
- No universal numeric thresholds.
- A technique must be invoked because a current text has matching evidence, not because the source book used it.

## Source provenance

- BOOK-01: 《射雕英雄传》 whole-book evidence settlement / candidate registry, 40/40 chapters.
- BOOK-02: 《诛仙》 whole-book evidence settlement / candidate registry, 256/256 chapters + epilogue.
- BOOK-03: 《盗墓笔记【壹】（七星鲁王 + 怒海潜沙）》 whole-book evidence settlement / candidate registry, 2 volumes / 75 numbered chapters.

Detailed source evidence remains in private project evidence. Public GitHub stores only abstracted techniques.

## RX-01 — Reward must open the next lock

**Source support:** BOOK-01 Supporting; BOOK-02 Supporting.

A meaningful gain, answer, relationship reward, victory or resource should not merely reduce pressure. Ask what new obligation, danger, uncertainty or choice it creates.

**Failure signal:** after a reward, the story becomes easier and reader curiosity drops.

**Production check:** `reward -> changed state -> new question/obligation/risk`.

---

## RX-02 — Fair clue, plausible wrong model, physical reclassification

**Source support:** BOOK-01 Supporting; BOOK-02 Formal.

Do not hide the decisive clue. Let real clues support a reasonable but incomplete explanation. Later evidence must visibly reclassify those same clues.

**Failure signal:** reveal depends on withheld author knowledge or a late confession rather than evidence already in play.

**Production check:** `real clue -> reasonable model -> contradiction -> verifiable reclassification`.

---

## RX-03 — Relationship reward through lived activity

**Source support:** BOOK-01 Supporting.

Food, jokes, games, errands and small habits can be reader rewards when they also change intimacy, permission, information or obligation.

**Failure signal:** banter is detachable decoration, or relationship is explained rather than lived.

---

## RX-04 — Build a realizable future before destroying it

**Source support:** BOOK-02 Formal.

Before major tragedy, make the reader able to picture the concrete near-future that could have happened. The loss should destroy an already believable path, not merely remove a person.

**Failure signal:** death or catastrophe arrives before the reader has emotionally invested in a specific possible tomorrow.

**Production check:** `credible future -> concrete next steps -> interruption -> irreversible loss -> aftershock`.

---

## RX-05 — Danger is sensed before it is named

**Source support:** BOOK-02 Supporting; BOOK-03 Supporting.

Let threat first alter sensory or behavioral reality before characters or narration label it.

**Failure signal:** narrator or expert announces danger before the reader experiences an anomaly.

---

## RX-06 — Body and behavior precede emotional explanation

**Source support:** BOOK-02 Supporting.

In high-emotion scenes, first let choice, hesitation, bodily limitation, object handling or silence carry the emotional contradiction. Explanation may follow only if something remains genuinely unavailable.

**Failure signal:** dialogue states the emotional result before the scene earns it; adding generic gestures does not count.

---

## RX-07 — False calm must change the next impact

**Source support:** BOOK-02 Supporting.

After sustained pressure, a concrete return to ordinary life can restore reader baseline. The next escalation should strike something made visible during that calm.

**Failure signal:** pressure remains at one constant volume or the calm is only filler.

---

## RX-08 — Temporary explanation must create the next testable contradiction

**Source support:** BOOK-03 Formal.

A mystery progresses through working models. Each useful explanation should close one question while leaving a located anomaly or producing a new testable prediction.

**Failure signal:** `strange event -> repeat strange event -> character says strange -> villain later explains`.

**Production check:** `current model -> supporting evidence -> remaining anomaly -> next verification action`.

---

## RX-09 — Local explanation, higher-level residual mystery

**Source support:** BOOK-03 Formal.

A physical or human explanation can remove one layer of uncertainty without emptying the entire mystery. The surviving higher-level question must have new evidence, not vague withholding.

**Failure signal:** mystery stays alive only because characters refuse to ask or author refuses to answer.

---

## RX-10 — Explanation pays an action/survival cost

**Source support:** BOOK-03 Formal.

Important information must alter route, time, resource, risk, authority, team division or immediate physical action when the scene is under pressure.

**Failure signal:** characters stop danger to deliver information and then continue exactly as before.

---

## RX-11 — Safety window can be reopened only by existing-chain evidence

**Source support:** BOOK-03 Supporting.

A temporary solution may be destabilized by new evidence, but that evidence must grow from an existing causal/object/information chain.

**Failure signal:** a new mystery is dropped in solely to prevent closure.

---

## RX-12 — Concealment breaks through testimony/object conflict

**Source support:** BOOK-03 Supporting.

Prefer exposing hidden truth because a statement fails against a physical trace, record, route or observed behavior.

**Failure signal:** knowledgeable character voluntarily gives a complete explanatory confession when pressure/evidence has not forced it.

---

## RX-13 — Reader propulsion is state-change plus desire-to-continue

**Cross-source synthesis.**

A scene can be causally correct and still fail. Track both:

1. what changed in the story;
2. what the reader now wants, fears, expects or needs answered.

**Failure signal:** events advance but the reader has no stronger reason to read the next paragraph/scene.

---

## RX-14 — Climax is option compression, not event volume

**Cross-source synthesis from source battle/choice patterns.**

A climax rises when options disappear, costs become personal, earlier assets/relationships return with changed meaning, and a choice becomes irreversible.

**Failure signal:** many attacks, arrivals or revelations occur, but the protagonist's meaningful choice set and emotional stakes stay flat.
