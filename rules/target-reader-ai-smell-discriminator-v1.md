# Target Reader AI-Smell Discriminator V1 — CALIBRATION GATE

## Status

This discriminator is target-reader calibrated and DEVELOPMENTAL.
It is not a generation checklist and must not be shown to a fresh prose generator as a list of things to imitate/avoid.

## Human authority

Actual target-reader evidence overrides model/evaluator preference for the candidate under review.

Phase378 established a directional model false positive:
- model predicted PROCESS lower AI smell;
- blinded human judged CONTROL lower AI smell and PROCESS very heavy / messy.

Until multiple independent fresh predictions match actual-human direction, model AI-smell evaluation is DIAGNOSTIC ONLY.

## Layer A — high-priority local conversational blockers

### A1. FUNCTIONALLY_EXACT_DIALOGUE_ADJACENCY_LADDER

Risk form:

`useful question -> exactly sufficient answer -> exactly useful follow-up -> exactly sufficient answer`

High risk requires context, not mere shortness. Look for repeated exchanges where:
- each turn has one clean authorial information function;
- the answer gives exactly what the next plot question needs;
- the next question arrives immediately and optimally;
- relationship, private agenda, misunderstanding, competing action, interruption, embarrassment, status or bodily task barely distorts the exchange;
- similar ladders recur within or across scenes.

Do NOT fail a passage merely because dialogue is short or because three turns form Q/A/Q.

Contrast requirement:
Before assigning HIGH, compare the same local function with surrounding action/POV/social behavior and, when available, actual-human positive/source prose.

### A2. SYNTHETIC SETUP/RETORT/NEXT-FUNCTION MACHINE

Retain the earlier human-prose calibration warning: neat setup -> neat reply -> next function becomes risky when repeated across characters/relationships.

Again, a single witty or compact exchange is not enough.

## Layer B — global organization signatures

Retain Phase377 as diagnostic evidence:
- reader-facing inference closure cadence;
- maximized functional density / no incidental life;
- uniform clue-inference-action cycle;
- character as task processor;
- causal neatness / executive-summary narrator;
- low social idiosyncrasy;
- engineered reveal escalation.

Important:
`LOWER_LAYER_B_RISK != HUMAN_PROSE_PASS`

Phase378 proves that reducing global optimization can still worsen AI smell.

## Layer C — coherence and reading viability

Score independently:
- local semantic continuity;
- immediate motive legibility;
- clarity/flow;
- desire to continue;
- skim/stop urge.

An anti-AI intervention FAILS if it reduces one diagnostic signature while materially worsening clarity or target-reader AI smell.

## Veto logic

Do not use a simple average across layers.

A repeated, actual-human-located Layer-A blocker may veto a model's aggregate preference even when several Layer-B signatures look improved.

No numeric threshold is currently authorized.

## Explicit non-rules

Never use these as mechanical AI detectors:
- short sentences = AI;
- one-line paragraphs = AI;
- explanation = AI;
- many concrete objects = AI;
- digression = human;
- messiness = human;
- slang/filler/stammer = human.

## Generation firewall

This file is for evaluator/calibration use.
A fresh prose generator must NOT receive:
- the signature names;
- target counts;
- forbidden-form checklists;
- instructions to deliberately create messiness, digression, incomplete speech or social noise.

A diagnosis may constrain a future experiment only through a separately designed process hypothesis, which must be fresh-tested and human-validated.

## Promotion gate

Before this discriminator can become a quality gate rather than diagnostic aid:
1. seal its directional prediction before human review;
2. test on multiple genuinely fresh independent candidate pairs;
3. obtain repeated agreement with actual target-reader AI-smell direction;
4. show no systematic clarity/continuation tradeoff;
5. record failures as immutable calibration evidence.

Until then:
- no YMGQ scale-up;
- no full rewrite;
- no architecture promotion;
- no accepted checkpoint advance.
