# Source Evidence Transfer Gate V1

Status: ACTIVE
Scope: novel-distillation source reading, book DNA, microcraft, cross-book promotion, and real-manuscript writing use.

## Purpose

A source-derived rule is not considered successfully distilled merely because the source was read, a mechanism was named, or a validator accepted it. The pipeline must separately prove:

1. SOURCE REALITY — the source really existed and was readable.
2. EVIDENCE TRACE — the mechanism is traceable to source-derived evidence with stable anchors/provenance.
3. DISTILLATION VALIDITY — the abstraction survives recurrence, ablation, specificity, de-duplication, and boundary tests.
4. WRITING TRANSFER — applying the mechanism measurably improves a new manuscript for the target reader.
5. SCALE TRANSFER — the improvement survives growth from short sample to longer continuous prose.

## Allowed verdicts

For every layer, only:
- REAL_EFFECTIVE
- PARTIALLY_EFFECTIVE
- UNPROVEN

No PASS at an earlier layer may substitute for a later layer.

## Mandatory provenance fields

Every source-backed candidate must bind:
- book_id / title
- raw source provider and current accessibility status
- source SHA-256 or equivalent immutable identifier
- source scope
- private evidence artifact ID
- source-derived anchor/sample IDs
- abstraction version
- failure/boundary conditions

Raw copyrighted text must not be copied into public Git.

## Transfer proof contract

Before any source-derived rule may be called WRITING_TRANSFER_PROVEN:

1. A new-manuscript target problem must be declared before authoring.
2. A baseline or pre-change span must exist.
3. The candidate rule/mechanism applied must be explicitly recorded.
4. The output must be read by the actual target reader.
5. The reader must report a directional improvement on the target dimension.
6. At least one adjacent quality dimension must not materially regress.
7. The same quality must survive at least one span-growth step.
8. AI-only evaluation cannot close the transfer gate.

## Human quality supremacy

Actual target-reader rejection overrides AI quality PASS for the current candidate. Historical AI results remain immutable audit records but cannot establish current human acceptance.

## Scale-transfer rule

Short-form success does not authorize full-manuscript generation.

Required progression:
SHORT CHOICE -> HUMAN SELECTION -> ONE BOUNDED SPAN -> HUMAN REVIEW -> NEXT SPAN

If quality collapses after span growth, classify SCALE_TRANSFER_FAIL and return to the last human-positive prose surface. Do not abstract the good surface away and regenerate freely.

## Mystery rule

Useful suspense may hide the larger truth; it may not hide the immediate objective required to understand the current action. Unknown immediate motive is not automatically suspense.

## Promotion restrictions

Book-level DNA, cross-book DNA, and Global DNA may be structurally valid yet have WRITING_TRANSFER = UNPROVEN.

No system-level or production-quality claim may be made from:
- synthetic benchmark success alone,
- router correctness alone,
- AI cold-read PASS alone,
- source coverage alone,
- DNA freeze alone.

At least one actual applied outcome with target-reader improvement is required for a writing-transfer claim.
