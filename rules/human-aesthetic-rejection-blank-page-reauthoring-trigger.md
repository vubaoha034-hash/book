# Human Aesthetic Rejection → Blank-Page Reauthoring Trigger

## Purpose

Prevent repeated micro-repair of a manuscript whose actual target reader rejects the dominant prose/scene/dialogue experience.

## Trigger

Trigger this rule when the actual human target reader reports any combination equivalent to:

- no meaningful desire to continue;
- manuscript feels like an event outline / event transcript rather than lived fiction;
- many separated dialogue scenes feel mechanically generated or share a portable cadence machine;
- reader would not recommend the manuscript;
- the reader disengages so early that suspense/climax cannot be meaningfully evaluated.

A prior AI PASS, PASS_WITH_FRICTION, gate compliance, causal correctness, or local dialogue grounding may NOT override this trigger.

## Required response

Once triggered:

1. STOP bounded line repair and patch-by-patch rewriting.
2. STOP treating previously preserved prose/scenes as default assets.
3. Classify the current manuscript as a labelled failed surface implementation.
4. Preserve only the minimal story kernel required for project identity.
5. Rebuild scene architecture from abstractions before generating new prose.
6. Permit merging/deleting/reordering characters, factions, subplots, suspense devices, deaths, reveals and scene order when that improves reader experience.
7. Do not line-edit or paraphrase old paragraphs into the new draft. Re-author scenes from scene goals, pressures, relationships and consequences.
8. Dialogue must be generated from character-specific desire, relationship asymmetry, current physical behavior and withheld information—not from a reusable Q&A/punchline cadence.
9. Before a full-manuscript rewrite, create a small representative prose pilot and require actual human target-reader approval.
10. If the human rejects the pilot, discard/rebuild the voice/scene approach before spending effort on a full draft.

## Anti-rationalization rule

The following are NOT sufficient reasons to preserve a mechanical passage:

- the dialogue is logically correct;
- the information is genuinely new;
- the exchange is concise;
- a prop/history reference makes the lines technically scene-specific;
- the same cadence is a running style;
- a fresh AI reader tolerated or liked it;
- the scene passes local G6D or natural-speech checks.

If the actual target reader experiences the pattern as repeated, mechanical, disgusting/unpleasant, or event-outline-like, that reader effect is controlling evidence for the labelled manuscript.

## Full-manuscript stop rule

Do not draft the entire replacement manuscript until the human has approved representative samples covering at least:

- opening/ordinary human interaction;
- high-pressure relationship dialogue;
- a suspense or reveal scene;
- a climax-adjacent scene.

This is a workflow gate, not a universal literary formula.

## Architecture promotion

A successful reauthoring of a labelled manuscript cannot by itself promote the general architecture/Skill. Generalization still requires a separate uncontaminated holdout.
