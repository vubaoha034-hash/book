# AI Smell Global Optimization Diagnostic V1

Status: DEVELOPMENTAL DIAGNOSTIC ONLY  
Effective from Phase377 settlement.

## Core failure family

`AI-F01_GLOBAL_AUTHORIAL_OPTIMIZATION_REGULARITY`

A prose surface may feel strongly AI-written even when it is readable, logical and propulsive if multiple locally reasonable techniques stay too consistently optimized for reader comprehension, clue delivery, causal closure and next-step progression.

This is a global / contiguous-span diagnostic. It is not a vocabulary blacklist.

## Supported high-risk signatures

Evaluate across a meaningful contiguous span, not sentence-by-sentence:

1. `AS-01 READER_FACING_INFERENCE_CLOSURE_CADENCE`
   - evidence is repeatedly followed by a neutral correct explanation of what it means or what now matters.
2. `AS-02 MAXIMIZED_FUNCTIONAL_DENSITY_NO_INCIDENTAL_LIFE`
   - almost every object/detail is immediately monetized as plot evidence or task progress.
3. `AS-03 UNIFORM_CLUE_INFERENCE_ACTION_CYCLE`
   - the same inspect/infer/act/reveal geometry repeats for several beats.
4. `AS-04 CHARACTER_AS_TASK_PROCESSOR`
   - character attention and questions are persistently optimized for the author's plot task rather than distorted by private/social priorities.
5. `AS-06 LOW_SOCIAL_IDIOSYNCRASY_GENERIC_VOICE`
   - speakers mainly carry information and can exchange lines/strategies with limited effect.

Supported amplifiers:

- `AS-05 CAUSAL_NEATNESS_AND_EXECUTIVE_SUMMARY_NARRATOR`
- `AS-07 ENGINEERED_REVEAL_ESCALATION`

## Explicit non-rules

Do NOT infer AI smell merely from:

- short sentences;
- one-line paragraphs;
- explanation;
- concrete objects;
- fast pace;
- cliffhangers;
- internal thought.

All occur in verified human source prose.

The risk is repeated global coupling and regularity.

## Diagnostic procedure

1. Lock the human-labelled span and its actual-human verdict.
2. Inspect at least 500–1500 Chinese characters where possible.
3. Mark recurring functional transitions, not just words.
4. For any claimed signature, require multiple located instances in the span or across human-labelled negative spans.
5. Compare against a verified source or human-positive counterexample.
6. Separate:
   - reader pull;
   - clarity;
   - tension;
   - AI smell.
   One may improve while another fails.
7. Return `SUPPORTED / PARTIAL / UNPROVEN` for each signature.

## Generator isolation

This diagnostic taxonomy must NOT be handed to a prose generator as a construction checklist by default.

Forbidden default workflow:

`show generator AS-01..AS-07 -> tell it to avoid all -> generate prose`

Reason: Phase373 showed that explicit technique activation can expose rule-shaped construction and worsen human reading response.

Preferred workflow:

`story/character facts -> isolated generator -> frozen prose -> separate diagnostic evaluator -> human blind comparison`

The generator should not receive target counts, beat geometry, paragraph-rhythm prescriptions, clue cadence, or the Phase377 taxonomy unless a later controlled experiment proves that exposure helps.

## Repair boundary

Do not assume a local symptom deletion repairs a global failure family.

Phase375 produced only a weak near-tie preference for a one-sentence repair; Phase376 valid replication returned a baseline win and both variants remained strongly AI-like.

Therefore:

- one-sentence deletion is secondary cleanup, not a primary AI-smell cure;
- multiple local edits may not be stacked without a new contract;
- process-level generation experiments require fresh human blind validation;
- no long-form propagation from this diagnostic alone.

## Human authority

Actual target-reader AI-smell judgment controls the current candidate.

`NO SKIM` does not mean `LOW AI SMELL`.

`WANTS TO CONTINUE` does not mean `NATURAL PROSE`.

No architecture promotion is authorized by this rule.
