# Reader-Experience Passive Validator Only V1

Effective from Phase374.

## Default mode

RX-C01 through RX-C08 are SOURCE_SUPPORTED hypotheses but are **not** proven generator recipes.

Default:
- direct prose-generation activation: OFF
- passive post-draft validation: ON
- architecture promotion: OFF
- long-form scale authorization: OFF

## Validator behavior

The validator may only flag a located defect already present in a naturally drafted short scene.

Allowed examples:
- immediate objective is not inferable;
- multiple consecutive beats do not change situation, option, cost, relation, evidence, or interpretation;
- danger is repeated without option/cost change;
- explanation is unsupported by in-scene evidence or need;
- character dialogue is logically exchangeable;
- scene exit does not alter the next action;
- surface beat pattern becomes visibly repetitive.

The validator must cite the located span and explain the defect in plain language.

## Forbidden behavior

The validator may not turn a source-derived pattern into visible prose scaffolding.

Forbidden by default:
- adding a countdown merely to increase pressure;
- adding new clues merely to increase state-change density;
- deleting connective explanation merely to appear less AI-like;
- forcing action beats between dialogue lines;
- multiplying mysteries to create suspense;
- withholding immediate character motive;
- prescribing a scene state machine for the author to translate into prose;
- activating multiple RX candidates as simultaneous construction targets.

## Repair contract

1. Natural draft first.
2. Passive validation second.
3. Pick at most one located reader-experience defect.
4. Make the smallest repair that addresses that defect.
5. Preserve all unrelated prose.
6. Human blind comparison controls acceptance.
7. A repair that does not beat baseline is rejected and cannot be scaled.

## Evidence boundary

Phase373 showed:
- CONTROL won overall preference, desire to continue, and least-AI judgment.
- RX_SURFACE stack increased tension but also confusion.
- RX_PULL stack triggered early skim urge.

Therefore direct activation is not validated.
