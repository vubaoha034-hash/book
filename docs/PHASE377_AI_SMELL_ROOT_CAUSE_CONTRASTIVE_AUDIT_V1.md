## 3. Main finding

The strongest supported root cause is not a vocabulary list and not a single sentence pattern.

It is:

# GLOBAL AUTHORIAL OPTIMIZATION REGULARITY

The prose repeatedly behaves as if an invisible controller is optimizing every local unit for reader comprehension, clue delivery, tension, and next-step progression.

The result can be logically clean and even readable, but it exposes the construction process.

A typical machine-shaped sequence is:

`anomaly -> relevant observation -> correct inference -> explicit closure -> purposeful action -> stronger anomaly`

When this sequence repeats across many paragraphs, the reader begins to feel the authoring algorithm even if every individual sentence is acceptable.

This is the central distinction missing from the old anti-AI workflow.

## 4. Ranked AI-smell signature taxonomy

### Tier A — strongest supported signatures

#### AS-01 Reader-facing inference closure cadence — HIGH

Problem:
Concrete evidence is repeatedly followed by a neutral sentence that tells the reader what the evidence means or what the current priority is.

Phase376 and Phase373 contain multiple located instances of this pattern. Phase364 also repeatedly closes character/plot meaning after behavior.

Source contrast shows that explanation itself is normal human prose. The risk comes from the repeated sequence:

`evidence -> correct neutral interpretation -> next plot function`

The narrator becomes an invisible case manager.

#### AS-02 Maximized functional density / no incidental life — HIGH

In the AI-heavy fresh samples, almost every object rapidly becomes evidence:

- ticket;
- numbered seat;
- bag;
- shoe;
- hospital paper;
- handwritten warning;
- phone message;
- family-linked photo;
- mud;
- key;
- costume.

This is efficient, but efficiency itself becomes visible.

The three real sources contain abundant material whose immediate function is not clue delivery: meals, jokes, social embarrassment, dialect, hierarchy, appetite, chores, memories and relationship play.

Important boundary:

This does **not** authorize random filler. The correction is not “add useless sentences.” The distinction is between an **inhabited detail** and an **author-planted evidence token**.

#### AS-03 Uniform clue-inference-action cycle — HIGH

The problem is not speed. It is repeated functional geometry.

When several consecutive units all perform the same transition—inspect, infer, decide, receive next clue—the prose begins to resemble a state machine.

Phase373 C made this especially visible through countdown-driven state transitions and won tension while losing human preference and causing an early skim urge.

The real sources vary scene function much more aggressively: humor can interrupt explanation; social embarrassment can delay plot movement; an argument can become character revelation; action can create information accidentally rather than because the scene needed the next clue.

#### AS-04 Character as task processor — HIGH

AI-heavy characters disproportionately:

- ask the useful question;
- inspect the relevant object;
- notice the plot-bearing detail;
- choose the useful next action;
- think in clean conditional branches.

They may be competent, but their attention appears optimized for the author's task.

The source characters filter information through appetite, pride, seniority, loyalty, embarrassment, prejudice, fear, affection and misunderstanding. Phase363's positive samples likewise gain force when old injury, body mechanics and relationship history interfere with optimal action.

The issue is therefore not “make characters stupid.” It is:

`personhood must distort task efficiency`.

#### AS-06 Low social idiosyncrasy / generic voice — HIGH

The Phase373 and Phase376 fresh characters often exchange useful information in broadly interchangeable syntax.

By contrast:

- 《盗墓笔记》 makes generation, dialect, temper and family hierarchy audible;
- 《诛仙》 makes gambling obsession,师兄弟 teasing and师父/师娘 authority change conversational behavior;
- 《射雕》 makes appetite, cleverness, bluntness, class and relationship change what a person says and how.

This does not support adding catchphrases or random slang. Voice is better modeled as:

`social position + relationship + private priority + decision strategy`.

### Tier B — supported amplifiers

#### AS-05 Causal neatness / executive-summary narrator — MEDIUM-HIGH

The generated narrator repeatedly states a compact, correct model of the current situation: what matters, what is safest, what someone is probably hiding, what a clue means.

Authoritative narration is not itself artificial; all three source books use it.

The risk is global regularity: the narrator is almost always correct, concise and optimally useful.

#### AS-07 Engineered reveal escalation — MEDIUM

Both Phase376 fresh scenarios escalate through a sequence of increasingly consequential evidence. Phase373 C used an even more explicit escalation device.

Escalation is legitimate and appears strongly in 《盗墓笔记》 as well. What differentiates the source is that escalation is contaminated—in a good sense—by dialect, mistakes, social conflict, crude humor, physical improvisation and uneven attention.

Therefore “more suspense” is not the repair. Continuous optimized escalation without lived interference is the risk.

## 5. Explicitly rejected simple rules

### AS-08 Short sentence / one-line paragraph = AI — NOT SUPPORTED

Phase376 heavy-AI samples use many short paragraphs, but Phase363 human-positive prose also uses them. 《盗墓笔记》 mixes short dialogue/action units with long paragraphs.

Do not ban short sentences.

### AS-09 Explanation = AI — NOT SUPPORTED

《射雕》 and 《诛仙》 both contain long explicit exposition. Human prose can explain extensively.

The stronger signature is repeated **reader-facing closure after evidence**, not explanation itself.

### AS-10 Many concrete objects = AI — NOT SUPPORTED AS A SIMPLE RULE

Phase363 positive prose is physically dense with bow, cart, wounds, wet stone and shields. Source novels also use vivid objects.

The distinction is whether objects are lived constraints / social objects / sensory reality, or whether nearly every object is planted to deliver the next clue.

## 6. Why the old anti-AI rules were insufficient

`rules/no-ai-smell.md` already identified many locally correct symptoms:

- over-explanation;
- average correctness;
- generic emotional gestures;
- manual-like dialogue;
- fake profundity;
- telegraphic pacing;
- voice convergence;
- dialogue template families.

Those observations should not be deleted.

The failure was mainly the level of intervention.

The previous method often assumed:

`locate bad local pattern -> delete/replace local pattern -> surface becomes more human`

Phase375/376 show that this inference is too strong.

The dominant smell can survive because the whole scene was generated under the same optimization geometry. Removing one sentence does not change the geometry.

In addition, Phase373 showed a second failure mode: if the generator is directly told to exhibit several desirable reader-experience properties, the rules themselves can become visible scaffolding. The prose becomes more engineered, not less.

Therefore:

`ANTI_AI_CHECKLIST_EXPOSED_TO_GENERATOR` is itself a risk factor.

## 7. Revised causal model

The current evidence supports the following model:

### Surface AI smell is often an emergent global property

It is produced by coupling among:

1. reader-facing inference closure;
2. maximized functional density;
3. repeated clue/inference/action architecture;
4. task-processor cognition;
5. neutral causal certainty;
6. low social idiosyncrasy;
7. engineered reveal cadence.

No single component is sufficient.

The stronger smell appears when several components stay aligned for too long.

This explains why:

- deleting one redundant sentence barely matters;
- adding more tension can worsen the prose;
- making motives clearer can improve readability without removing AI smell;
- a scene can have no skim point and still feel strongly AI-written.

## 8. Relation to Phase363–370

Phase363 should not be reclassified as fully non-AI; the historical human feedback was broad positive reading response, not a clean isolated AI-smell rating.

However, it provides useful directional contrast:

- physical limitation changes action;
- relationship history changes tactical choice;
- characters coordinate before explaining;
- action produces information as a side effect;
- not every beat exists to hand the reader a clue.

Phase369/370 also cannot be called AI-smell solved. They demonstrate that pull, motive clarity and AI smell are partially separable dimensions.

This is important for future tests: do not use “I want to continue” as a proxy for natural prose.

## 10. Required next experiment — Phase378

The next experiment must test a **generation process change**, not another surface checklist.

Recommended design:

1. Use a genuinely fresh story situation.
2. Freeze story facts and character-state facts before prose generation.
3. Generator receives only world/character/continuity facts required to write the scene.
4. Generator must **not** receive the Phase377 AI-smell taxonomy, RX candidate list, desired clue cadence, desired paragraph rhythm or a target count of techniques.
5. Generator writes from one bounded lived viewpoint / local social situation without a beat-by-beat state machine.
6. Freeze the draft.
7. A separate evaluator then applies Phase377 only after drafting.
8. If a global optimization signature is detected, do not line-edit many symptoms. Produce at most one process-level alternate draft from the same facts.
9. Blind actual-human compare.
10. Judge AI smell separately from desire-to-continue and skim behavior.

Because the current chat has already seen the Phase377 taxonomy, a same-context generation comparison cannot be treated as a clean independent proof. A valid promotion-grade test requires a fresh independent generation context or equivalent isolation.
