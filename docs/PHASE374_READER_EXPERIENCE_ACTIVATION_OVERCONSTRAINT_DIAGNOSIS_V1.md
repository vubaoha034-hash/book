## Diagnosis

### D1 — Observational truth was converted into visible prose machinery

Phase372 candidates describe patterns observed across source fiction. They are useful as descriptions and possible validators. Phase373 treated them too directly as construction instructions.

This exposed the rule shape on the page.

### D2 — RX_SURFACE produced tension at a clarity cost

A deliberately emphasized:
- evidence before explanation,
- withheld response,
- voice through decision style,
- short/varied surface beats.

Human result: strongest tension, but also very confusing.

The problem is not that evidence-first or differentiated decision style is false. The problem is excessive **author-side suppression of connective tissue**. The reader had to infer too many local links at once.

Important correction:
`less explicit explanation != less AI-like`.

The control B was judged least AI-like despite being more explicit and smoother. Therefore anti-AI quality for this reader depends strongly on natural semantic continuity, not maximal compression or withholding.

### D3 — RX_PULL became a visible state machine

C deliberately emphasized:
- clear goal,
- frequent state change,
- option-loss pressure,
- explicit countdown.

The repeated countdown (seven lines, six, four, three, two, final line) made the pressure mechanism visible as authorial scaffolding. Additional clue turns accumulated rapidly.

Human result: motives readable, but skim urge appeared after a few lines and the version felt ordinary.

Important correction:
`state-change density must be measured, not theatrically displayed`.

A scene can have meaningful state changes without announcing a timer or producing a new clue on every beat.

### D4 — Candidate stacking is unsafe

Each experimental version activated three candidates simultaneously. That prevents clean attribution and encourages overdetermined prose.

Future probes must activate at most one major candidate family at a time, and preferably one candidate at a time when practical.

### D5 — Direct generation and passive validation must be separated

Source-derived reader-experience knowledge now has two possible roles:

1. GENERATOR DIRECTIVE — tells the author how to construct the scene.
2. PASSIVE VALIDATOR — checks an already natural draft for located failures.

Phase373 rejects broad direct activation as currently implemented.

Default for RX-C01–RX-C08 is changed to:
`PASSIVE_VALIDATOR_ONLY / GENERATOR_DIRECTIVE_DISABLED`.

This does not invalidate the source evidence. It invalidates the current calling mode.

## New calling rules

1. Draft must first be written naturally from character, situation and causal need without exposing RX labels or targets to the prose generator.
2. RX candidates inspect the completed short draft afterward.
3. A validator may flag only a located failure, e.g.:
   - reader cannot infer current objective,
   - several beats pass with no meaningful state change,
   - pressure repeats without changing options/cost,
   - explanation arrives without evidence or scene need,
   - character lines are exchangeable,
   - surface beat pattern becomes mechanically repetitive.
4. Validator may not prescribe a replacement scene algorithm.
5. Repair must be local and minimal. No automatic insertion of countdowns, extra clues, extra action beats or deliberate withholding merely to satisfy a rule.
6. Maximum one reader-experience failure repaired per iteration unless failures are causally identical.
7. Actual-human comparison remains controlling evidence.
