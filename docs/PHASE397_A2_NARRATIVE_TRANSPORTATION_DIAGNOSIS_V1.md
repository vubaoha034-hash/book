## Primary diagnosis

The current blocker is not lack of plot information and not merely insufficient emotional vocabulary.

Working failure family: NT-F01_EVENT_TRACE_VISIBLE_THROUGH_PROSE

The A2 prose exposes the underlying event simulation too directly. The reader can feel the author advancing through prepared beats rather than inhabiting a continuous lived scene.

### Evidence in A2

A2 repeatedly resolves one beat per paragraph or fragment:
- “他停住了。”
- “灵牌上的名字，是严九。”
- “还是那两个字。”
- “严九。”
- “是苏荷。”
- “她正守着灵。”
- “韩敬。”
- “这一次，他明白了。”
- “苏荷看见他了。”
- “她不是没有认出他。”
- “她是在叫他不要进去。”

Individually these can work. In aggregate they reveal designed emphasis and produce storyboard/cinematic-card cadence.

The scene also repeatedly closes the reader's inference after the action already carried it:
- action/sight -> explanation of what it means -> next beat;
- physical reaction -> narrator restates emotional conclusion;
- clue -> protagonist summarizes its significance.

This creates AI-like authorial optimization even though the plot is coherent.

## Contrast with historical human-positive prose

The Phase394 human-positive anchor and Phase370 positive span differ in three important ways:

1. Continuous external pressure: events overlap. Action, danger, speech, relationship and new information happen at the same time rather than waiting for one beat to finish before the next starts.
2. Characters own independent priorities: a person does not exist only to reveal the next clue. Their physical objective, anger, injury, loyalty, fear or immediate problem continues while information emerges.
3. Meaning is often left inside action: prose lets the reader infer why a person stopped, changed direction, protected someone or misunderstood something instead of repeatedly summarizing the intended interpretation.

## Secondary diagnosis

NT-F03_PREMISE_SUSPENSE_WITHOUT_SCENE_PLEASURE

The premise creates curiosity, but curiosity alone does not create forward desire. The scene provides little additional reading pleasure from character voice, social texture, relationship chemistry, unpredictable human behavior, ordinary-life contrast, or locally satisfying mini-payoffs.

Therefore human reports “有悬念” and “不想继续看” simultaneously. These are not contradictory.

## Next bounded repair direction

Do NOT simply add more emotion words, more internal monologue, a memory/exposition block about the marriage, more one-line emphasis paragraphs, more mysteries, or more diagnostic rules directly to the writer.

For the next attempt, repair only three things:
1. Continuous lived scene instead of one-beat-per-paragraph rendering. Combine concurrent action, perception, relationship and environmental pressure. Event-card boundaries must not become prose boundaries.
2. One or two ordinary relationship-specific details in present action. No backstory dump.
3. Leave more meaning unclosed. Let reader inference survive across paragraphs.
