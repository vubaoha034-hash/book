# MICROCRAFT Epistemic Scope + Pattern Family Density V1

Status: IMPLEMENTED_PENDING_FREEZE_AND_FRESH_VALIDATION
Phase: 345

## Why this exists

Repeated correctly-bound fresh audits exposed two different failure modes in the previous microcraft benchmark:

1. object-level knowledge was being flattened with knowledge-about-knowledge;
2. a locally plausible/removable joke was being treated as a hard dialogue failure when the stronger literary risk was manuscript-level repetition of the same cadence across characters.

Phase344 therefore calibrated the labels. Phase345 implements that calibration without rewriting historical Attempt1–5 results.

## G6D: epistemic scope

G6D now distinguishes:

```text
OBJECT_FACT
LISTENER_KNOWS_OBJECT_FACT
PUBLIC_KNOWLEDGE_OF_OBJECT_FACT
REPORTED_RUMOR_CONTENT
EVIDENCE_OF_EXPOSURE_OR_SPREAD
ACTIONABLE_RISK_FROM_PUBLIC_KNOWLEDGE
```

Knowing X does not imply knowing that outsiders know X.

A known object fact can be repeated as embedded content of a new meta-proposition such as “the rumor outside says X about you.” That repetition is not automatically reader-only exposition.

Hard `AUTHOR_INFORMATION_MOUTHPIECE_FAIL` remains for pure shared setup with no distinct object/meta-knowledge/risk/relationship/task payload.

Unsupported public-knowledge claims default to HOLD rather than reviewer invention.

## G6D: calibrated local banter

Rhetorical-ladder harvesting and ATS parity remain mandatory.

Self-cancelling assertion, induced follow-up, turn deletion and portability remain diagnostics, but removability alone is not a hard-fail rule.

A local hard dialogue-template failure requires independent evidence that the exchange is ungrounded or materially misleading in its own scene.

Locally plausible but portable cadence is routed to G8 as a pattern-family candidate.

## G8: dialogue pattern family density

G8 clusters dialogue by functional/cadence skeleton across the full manuscript.

Risk rises when the same family:

- recurs in separated scenes;
- crosses different speaker pairs/relationships;
- uses similar reveal timing and cadence;
- depends weakly on current objects, tasks, shared history or status;
- can be transferred between unrelated characters with little semantic change;
- causes cross-character voice homogenization.

Risk code:

`DIALOGUE_TEMPLATE_FAMILY_DENSITY_RISK`

The current purposive evidence does not justify a universal numeric threshold. No rule such as “N occurrences = FAIL” is frozen.

## False-positive protection

Do not flag merely because:

- the manuscript contains many jokes;
- short Q&A appears often;
- one character has a supported habitual cadence;
- a relationship has a running joke;
- several scenes contain humor generated by different concrete mechanisms.
