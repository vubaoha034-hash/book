# MICROCRAFT Dialogue Coverage + Social Speech Hardening V1.2

## Trigger

A correctly bound Fresh Context V3-G6D V1.1 regression on the unchanged historical manuscript rediscovered 2 of 3 pre-registered failures:

- exact temporal semantic-chain failure: rediscovered;
- exact dialogue ping-pong failure: rediscovered;
- pre-registered public-background author-mouthpiece bundle: missed.

The same evaluator instead flagged a directly responsive first-hand anecdote as author-mouthpiece, creating a false-positive risk.

Therefore V1.1 is not promoted.

## Diagnosis

Two separate errors remained:

1. **coverage / selection failure** — the audit did not guarantee that every ordinary multi-fact public-background turn would be harvested before judgment;
2. **discrimination failure** — listener practical need was weighted too heavily, so a legitimate social anecdote could be misclassified as exposition.

## V1.2 changes

### 1. Candidate harvest before judgment

Regression and pre-delivery audits must inventory every:

- 3+ fact dialogue turn;
- current-event / listener-role / reward / rumor / arrivals bundle;
- listener-own-mission restatement;
- rhetorical ladder;
- ordinary early/mid-scene plot-setup turn;
- possible social anecdote.

No unexplained omission is allowed for PASS.

### 2. Public-background bundle discriminator

Current-plot exposition is high risk when a speaker tells an experienced listener several facts the listener already knows about:

- the event;
- timing;
- the listener's role;
- reward/stakes;
- rumor spread;
- outsider arrivals.

One newly actionable fact does not automatically justify the rest of the packet.

### 3. Social anecdote carveout

A first-hand anecdote can be natural even when most details are not task-essential. It may PASS when it directly responds to a question, the speaker plausibly owns the story, the relationship permits casual sharing, and the details carry voice/relationship rather than current-plot orientation.

This carveout exists to preserve living fiction rather than optimizing all speech into task messages.

### 4. Mixed bundle handling

If a line combines social anecdote with public setup, classify MIXED and audit the setup claims separately.

### 5. Coverage evidence

The final G6D record must expose candidate inventory count, audited count, omissions, public-background bundle candidates, social-anecdote candidates and rhetorical-ladder candidates.

## Acceptance

The next fresh/no-hint manuscript regression must:

- use immutable V1.2 machinery;
- keep benchmark cases hidden;
- use the unchanged historical manuscript;
- independently rediscover all 3 pre-registered seed failures;
- avoid treating legitimate social anecdote/relationship dialogue as author-mouthpiece;
- preserve natural protected scenes.

Repeated fresh attempts on unchanged machinery until a favorable random result remains forbidden.
