## A. Residual AI smell — deeper working diagnosis

Phase382 correctly located over-regularized Q&A cadence, but that is only one visible instance. Current broader family:

`AI-F03_EXCESSIVE_AUTHORIAL_EXPLICITNESS_AND_DESIGNED_CLOSURE`

This is a labelled-manuscript working diagnosis, not a universal literary law.

### AI-F03.1 Narrator closes the inference too often

Pattern:

`event / gesture / line -> narrator immediately states what it means emotionally or strategically`

The reader is rarely allowed to infer the subtext alone. This resembles an assistant explaining a scene after generating it.

Risk signals:
- “这句话不是……而是……”
- “他终于明白……” immediately after already-readable evidence
- “真正让他难受的是……”
- “现在他要的不是……而是……”
- explanatory emotional summary immediately after a strong concrete beat

Do not ban these phrases mechanically. Diagnose repeated explanatory closure across a span.

### AI-F03.2 Too many engineered quotable contrasts

The prose repeatedly creates polished contrast forms, reversals and last-line epigrams. Each can work locally, but repeated use makes author design visible.

Examples of geometry, not banned wording:
- three years ago X / now Y;
- wanted to kill him / now must protect him;
- not afraid of death / afraid of truth;
- one compact thematic sentence closes the paragraph/scene.

Human popular fiction usually allows more connective, awkward, ordinary and unresolved prose between peaks.

### AI-F03.3 Characters possess excessive emotional self-knowledge

Characters too often understand exactly what they feel, why they feel it, and which contradiction defines them. This produces a “therapist narrator” effect.

More human texture may include:
- wrong attribution;
- avoidance;
- focus on a trivial concrete thing;
- delayed realization;
- shame expressed through another complaint;
- emotion that changes behavior before the character can name it.

### AI-F03.4 Functional density remains too high

Almost every object, memory, interruption and sentence is useful to plot, theme, clue, emotion or causality. The scene lacks incidental life.

This extends existing AI-F01/AS-02. Popular long fiction often contains small social, comic, sensory, habitual or petty details that are not immediately monetized as plot progress but increase world/character reality.

### AI-F03.5 Scene turns are too clean

The current prose often follows:

`question -> evidence -> correct inference -> emotional update -> decision`

Even after dialogue becomes more natural, this underlying geometry remains model-like because everyone reaches the story-useful next state efficiently.

### AI-F03.6 Uniform tonal seriousness

Current YMGQ passages spend too much time in grief, guilt, pursuit, testimony and moral weight. Distinctive character pleasure, humor, pettiness, social friction, appetite, vanity, habit, embarrassment and ordinary desire are underused.

This reduces voice differentiation and makes every character feel authored by the same serious intelligence.

## B. Low desire-to-continue — separate root diagnosis

Working family:

`RP-F03_LOW_FORWARD_DESIRE_DESPITE_LOCAL_COHERENCE`

### RP-F03.1 The dominant question is retrospective

Current pull is mainly:

“What happened three years ago?”
“Who falsified the record?”
“Where did the grain go?”

These can create curiosity, but once the answer direction becomes predictable, forward pressure collapses.

Need a question with uncertain future consequence:
- what will this person choose;
- who will betray whom;
- what will happen before the deadline;
- which relationship will break;
- what newly discovered rule changes survival;
- what answer, if true, forces a different action now.

### RP-F03.2 Reader can already predict the next several beats

Current likely chain is easy to forecast:

keep Gu alive -> obtain testimony -> escape pursuers -> expose old crime / reach bridge.

If the reader can forecast the function of the next pages, new facts feel like filling in a form rather than opening a door.

### RP-F03.3 Anonymous danger is not enough

Arrows, pursuers, doors and route pressure create motion but not necessarily desire. Without an intelligent adversary with a specific intention, danger behaves like weather.

A strong third force should change the meaning of the choice, not merely interrupt dialogue.

### RP-F03.4 Character identification improved, transportation did not rise enough

The human response indicates Chen Changling became more emotionally real, but this did not produce sustained immersion/page-turn compulsion.

This matches narrative research distinguishing character identification from transportation. A reader can care about a person and still not feel compelled by the unfolding plot.

### RP-F03.5 Current cast lacks a strong “I want to spend time with them” engine

Popular reference works do not rely only on mystery:
- memorable personality contrast;
- relationship chemistry;
- humor/banter with specific history;
- competence/oddity/charisma;
- ordinary life the reader later misses.

Current characters are becoming emotionally credible but remain heavily defined by the case/pursuit task.

### RP-F03.6 Rewards are mostly explanation rewards

Many current payoffs are facts: a true name, a false military record, a grain-account explanation. The reader needs more varied rewards:
- relationship reversal;
- competence reveal;
- unexpected alliance;
- concrete gain with a new cost;
- emotional wish briefly made possible;
- physical/world anomaly that reclassifies what came before.

## C. Comparison with popular-reference mechanisms

### 《盗墓笔记》 mechanism

The public premise already stacks: old disaster -> inherited record -> expedition -> concrete anomalies -> identity/structure questions. Each answer has potential to expose a larger physical mystery. Pull repeatedly shifts forward rather than only reconstructing the past.

### 《射雕英雄传》 mechanism

Public summaries/reviews repeatedly emphasize memorable, sharply differentiated characters together with lively plot. The reader can want the next encounter because of the people, not only because of a mystery answer.

### 《庆余年》 mechanism

Public reader response repeatedly describes being kept waiting for updates and wanting to know how the story ends. Long-range questions are tied to identity, family, power, loyalties and major future choices, while humor and character interaction vary the tonal surface.

### 《诡秘之主》 mechanism

Public reviews emphasize world-detail credibility, ordinary-life attachment, homesickness/adaptation, surprising arc difficulty and layered setting discovery. The story does not keep every paragraph at maximum plot efficiency; ordinary life increases later loss and immersion.

## D. Production conclusion

Do NOT continue the current scene merely by adding more testimony, grain-account detail, pursuers or internal monologue.

Do NOT try to fix AI smell with a phrase blacklist.

The next bounded experiment should change scene architecture before prose:

1. preserve Chen's successful emotional reality;
2. reduce explanatory narrator closure;
3. reduce polished contrast/end-line frequency;
4. introduce non-task character behavior and relationship texture;
5. create one strong prospective question whose answer changes immediate action;
6. add an agentic third force whose move is surprising but causally grounded;
7. ensure one answer opens a more valuable forward problem rather than merely closing history;
8. provide at least one reward other than explanation;
9. deliver only a short representative pilot for actual-human judgment before scaling.
