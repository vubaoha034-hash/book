# Emotional Staging & Scene Voltage Ledger V1

## Purpose

Catch scenes whose facts and dialogue are correct but whose emotion is only stated or skeletonized.

## Required record for high-emotion beats

- emotional_debt_entering_scene
- immediate_physical_or_social_pressure
- character_want_A
- conflicting_want_B
- what_character_refuses_to_say
- concrete behavior carrying the conflict
- dialogue_turn_that_changes_power_or_understanding
- irreversible_or_costly_choice
- aftershock
- reader_emotion_before
- reader_emotion_after
- verdict

## Failure codes

`EMOTIONAL_SCENE_REDUCED_TO_EVENT_SKELETON`
: important emotion is carried mostly by functional short turns with insufficient pressure, resistance, behavior, subtext or aftershock.

`EMOTIONAL_PAYOFF_NOT_DRAMATIZED`
: the text reports a relationship/emotional result without making the reader experience the transition.

`GENERIC_ACTION_BEAT_IS_NOT_STAGING`
: adding glances, sighs, clenched hands or pauses without changing choice/read does not repair the scene.

## False-positive controls

Minimal dialogue may pass when silence, physical constraint, shared object or relationship history makes the omitted emotion strongly legible.

Do not require lyrical prose or longer scenes. Require dramatic pressure and readable change.
