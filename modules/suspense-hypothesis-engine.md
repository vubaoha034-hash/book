# Suspense Hypothesis Engine V1

## Purpose

Distinguish a real suspense engine from merely withholding an explanation.

## Required chain

For each material mystery:

- lock: what is concretely unknown?
- stake: why does the answer matter now?
- current_reader_model: what can the reader reasonably think?
- evidence_for_model
- fair_false_key_or_competing_model
- remaining_anomaly
- character_validation_action
- cost_of_validation
- result
- model_update
- larger_lock_after_partial_unlock

## Failure codes

`SUSPENSE_HAS_NO_WORKING_HYPOTHESIS`
: anomalies exist but the reader cannot form a meaningful model.

`SUSPENSE_REPEATS_ANOMALY_WITHOUT_ESCALATION`
: the same strange sign repeats without changing theory, action, cost or stakes.

`SUSPENSE_PAYOFF_IS_EXPLANATION_DUMP`
: later dialogue/confession supplies the causal model that the prior evidence did not let the reader actively build/test.

`SUSPENSE_UNLOCK_CREATES_NO_LARGER_PRESSURE`
: answer closes pressure without new consequence or higher-level question.

## False-positive control

Not every uncertainty needs a multi-stage mystery. Use this gate only for suspense the manuscript asks the reader to carry across scenes.

## Source-backed operating principle

Temporary explanations should create a testable next contradiction; local explanation may close one layer while leaving a higher-level mystery only when new evidence supports that residual question.
