# Scene Entry Motivation V1

## Purpose

Prevent important characters from entering, returning, leaving, or changing roles only because the plot currently needs their function.

## Required record for a material entry or re-entry

- prior location or commitment
- newly perceived condition
- information or trigger source
- active obligation or desire
- plausible alternative action
- why this character acts now
- cost or tradeoff of entering
- first action after entry
- downstream scene change
- verdict

## Failure codes

`MAJOR_SCENE_ENTRY_HAS_NO_CAUSAL_TRIGGER`
: a material character re-enters or changes participation without a located perception, report, obligation, remembered debt, changed condition, or other supported trigger.

`AUTHOR_CONVENIENCE_REENTRY`
: the only supported explanation for the entry is that the scene benefits from the character being present.

## False-positive controls

Routine background movement does not need explicit motivation. Re-entry may be inferred when an earlier scene established an ongoing obligation and the new action is its direct continuation.

## Revision rule

Prefer planting a causal seed before the entry rather than explaining motivation afterward.
