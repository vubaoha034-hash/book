# Natural Speech Under Pressure V1

## Purpose

G6D asks whether a line is motivated and true. This module asks whether a human in this pressure, relationship and knowledge state would package the information this way.

## Required record for high-information dialogue

- immediate_pressure
- speaker_priority_now
- listener_already_knows
- minimum_new_payload
- likely_omissions
- interruption_or_inference_opportunities
- clause_packaging
- vocabulary_fit_to_role
- sentence_completeness_fit_to_pressure
- alternative_carriers_available
- verdict

## Failure codes

`SPEECH_PACKAGE_DOES_NOT_MATCH_HUMAN_PRESSURE`
: a character bundles multiple facts/meta-facts/conclusions into a polished explanatory package that is locally useful but implausibly complete for the live situation.

`CHARACTER_SPEAKS_IN_AUTHOR_SUMMARY_UNITS`
: speech is organized by what the reader needs summarized rather than what the speaker would prioritize first.

`DIALOGUE_HAS_NO_HUMAN_OMISSION_OR_INFERENCE`
: repeated high-information speech leaves nothing for listener inference despite shared context or pressure.

## Repair discipline

Do not simply add slang, particles, stutters or gestures. Reorder information by urgency and relationship. Allow omission, interruption, partial answer, misunderstanding, environmental proof or later completion when supported.

## False-positive controls

Formal testimony, briefing, command, confession or deliberate persuasion may legitimately be complete if the scene gives the speaker time, motive and audience need.
