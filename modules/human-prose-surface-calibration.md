# Human Prose Surface Calibration — Developmental

Purpose: prevent a logically valid manuscript from being expanded or rewritten at full length while its actual target reader rejects the prose/dialogue surface.

## Trigger

Use this calibration layer when:
- an actual target reader reports strong language-surface rejection;
- AI/fresh-audit judged the same surface natural or acceptable;
- the disagreement is manuscript-wide rather than one isolated factual error.

## Core non-equivalences

```text
SHORT != NATURAL
CONCISE != HUMAN
WITTY != CHARACTER-SPECIFIC
SCENE-SUPPORTED != PLEASANT_TO_READ
LOCAL_DIALOGUE_LOGIC != GOOD_PROSE
QUOTABLE_LINE != EMOTIONAL_TRUTH
FAST_EVENT_EXCHANGE != PAGE-TURN PULL
```

## Synthetic short-turn failure family

Warning signs include repeated combinations of:
1. question -> exact short answer -> follow-up question -> exact short answer;
2. setup -> neat witty retort -> next plot function;
3. child/supporting character -> question -> compact aphoristic summary of protagonist;
4. every turn has one clean author-defined function and lands without overlap, hesitation, mishearing, deflection, physical interruption or unfinished priority;
5. characters across different relationships share the same dry, clever, clipped cadence;
6. dialogue replaces lived action instead of arising from it;
7. emotional knowledge is summarized in a polished line instead of becoming observable through what people do, avoid, misunderstand, or cannot finish saying.

No single short line is a failure. The blocker is manuscript-level dominance plus actual-reader rejection.

## Calibration-before-scale rule

When triggered:
- DO NOT immediately rewrite the full manuscript.
- Select 2-4 representative failed scenes plus at least one human-positive anchor scene.
- Rewrite only the failed samples.
- Preserve plot facts and character identity.
- Produce a human calibration pack.
- The actual target reader must judge the samples before the style is propagated.

If no sample reaches human acceptance, continue sample calibration instead of full-manuscript rewriting.

## Rewrite principles

Prefer:
- uneven turn lengths driven by speaker priority, not quotas;
- physical action and perception carrying information that dialogue need not state;
- asymmetrical conversation: one person may evade, ignore, misunderstand, interrupt, or refuse the offered conversational frame;
- silence that changes behavior, not decorative pauses;
- character-specific diction rooted in social role, relationship and current pressure;
- dialogue embedded inside continuous action rather than line-by-line ping-pong;
- emotional inference from behavior rather than polished character-summary lines;
- protagonist agency and visible consequence when the scene's function permits it.

Avoid fake-naturalism:
- random filler particles;
- forced stammering;
- arbitrary slang;
- lengthening every line;
- decorative body language;
- deliberately making grammar messy without character/pressure cause.

## Quantitative diagnostics

Dialogue length, short-turn ratios, repeated cadence families and turn-role distributions may support diagnosis.
They are not quotas and cannot independently establish PASS/FAIL.

## Human authority

If a fresh AI calls a surface natural but the actual target reader reports strong rejection, the human result controls the current candidate.
The AI result remains historical evidence and is not rewritten.
