# Climax Experiential Voltage V1

## Purpose

Separate structural escalation from the reader's actually experienced crescendo.

A climax may correctly remove options and reactivate prior assets while still reading flat because scene realization, anticipation, emotional compression or consequence delivery is weak.

## Required record

For each major climax segment, record both STRUCTURE and EXPERIENCE.

### Structural layer
- options removed;
- stakes added;
- prior asset/relationship reactivated;
- irreversible choice/cost.

### Experiential layer
- what the reader is anticipating before the beat;
- whether the beat feels larger/more personal/more irreversible than the prior beat;
- bodily or emotional pressure created by scene realization, not merely by summary of facts;
- whether the prose slows, sharpens or focuses at the moment where consequence should land;
- whether the reader would care if this beat were compressed to a one-line event summary;
- after-impact on the next scene;
- actual fresh-reader voltage judgment.

## Failure codes

`STRUCTURAL_ESCALATION_WITHOUT_EXPERIENTIAL_CRESCENDO`
: option compression and stakes are present on paper, but the sequence does not produce a corresponding felt rise in tension/emotion/anticipation.

`CLIMAX_BEATS_READ_AS_EQUIVALENT_EVENT_UNITS`
: multiple nominally escalating beats have similar surface weight and emotional intensity, flattening the crescendo.

`PAYOFF_CONSEQUENCE_LANDS_AS_SUMMARY_NOT_EVENT`
: the irreversible result is important in story logic but is experienced mainly as information.

## False-positive controls

A restrained climax may pass. Loud prose, longer paragraphs, more combat and exclamation are not required.

The test is relative felt escalation and concentration of meaning, not spectacle.

## Core distinction

`OPTION_COMPRESSION != EXPERIENCED_VOLTAGE`.
