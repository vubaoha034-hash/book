## 3. What materially improved

### HPI-01 Emotion participates in causality

The improvement did not come from simply adding emotion words. Chen Changling's grief, shame, hatred, guilt and need for truth began to alter choices:

- he wants Gu Chengyue dead but needs him alive as a witness;
- learning the truth threatens Chen's own remembered version of his brother;
- the brother is experienced as a concrete person from memory, not merely as a plot fact;
- emotional conflict changes whether Chen draws, lowers or withholds the knife.

Production implication: emotion must modify speech, concealment, attention, action, cost or decision. Do not treat interiority as decorative commentary added after information exchange.

### HPI-02 Character-first information delivery

The better passages reveal facts through what the answer means to a person. Information is no longer automatically the primary unit of scene construction.

Production implication: when a reveal arrives, first ask whose self-image, relationship, hope, guilt or decision it changes. The scene is not complete merely because the reader now knows the fact.

### HPI-03 Subjective memory creates lived personhood

Specific remembered behavior of Chen Xiaoliu improved emotional reality because the memory belonged to the relationship and changed the present experience. This is different from generic backstory or a neutral biography packet.

Production implication: memory is useful only when it is something this person would remember now and it changes present perception/action.

## 4. Current primary open AI-smell defect

Actual human located this exchange as strongly AI-like:

> “埋了。”
>
> “哪儿？”
>
> “不知道。”

The problem is NOT “short sentences are AI”. The existing Phase377 diagnostic already forbids that simplistic rule.

Current best diagnosis:

`AI-F02_OVERREGULARIZED_AUTHOR_SERVING_DIALOGUE_CADENCE` (current labelled-manuscript working diagnosis; not a universal promoted rule)

Observed surface:

- prompt → exact minimal answer → exact follow-up → exact closure;
- every turn performs one clean authorial function;
- speakers cooperate too perfectly with the author's pacing and information order;
- little interference from memory, shame, anger, status, misunderstanding, reluctance, current physical task or personal speech habit;
- the exchange can resemble an interrogation transcript or screenplay beat machine rather than lived speech;
- terse turn geometry becomes visibly designed when repeated.

This is related to existing `AI-F01_GLOBAL_AUTHORIAL_OPTIMIZATION_REGULARITY`, especially AS-04 CHARACTER_AS_TASK_PROCESSOR and AS-06 LOW_SOCIAL_IDIOSYNCRASY_GENERIC_VOICE, but the human-located symptom is narrower and more actionable for this labelled manuscript.

## 5. Anti-overcorrection

Do NOT turn this into any of the following universal bans:

- short dialogue is forbidden;
- one-line paragraphs are forbidden;
- every answer must be long;
- every line needs hesitation/filler;
- every dialogue beat needs an action tag;
- add more psychological exposition everywhere;
- make dialogue deliberately messy for its own sake.

Natural terse dialogue can remain when pressure, relationship and voice make it inevitable. The failure is repeated author-serving regularity, not brevity itself.

## 6. Current production correction

For the next bounded prose pilot:

1. Preserve the successful emotional/subjective direction.
2. Do not use the Phase381 rejected prose as default wording or sequence.
3. When one character asks for information, the other character may answer according to what they remember, fear, resent, hide or can bear to say — not according to the cleanest information packet.
4. Permit partial answers, unwanted details, defensive reframing, socially loaded silence, mistaken emphasis and interruption only where character-specific pressure naturally causes them.
5. Avoid repeated `short question → exact short answer → short follow-up → reveal` ladders across the span.
6. Do not give the generator an exhaustive anti-AI checklist or target counts; these notes are coordinator calibration evidence, not a prose-construction template.
7. Actual human reading remains the acceptance authority.
