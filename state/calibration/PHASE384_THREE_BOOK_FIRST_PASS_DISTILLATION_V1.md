## A. Immediate correction to the prior project model

The three books do NOT succeed through one universal hook formula.

- 《诡秘之主》: question reclassification + identity performance + ordinary-life grounding + recurring social system + mystery escalation.
- 《凡人修仙传》: clear personal desire + cautious agency + resource conversion + progression + risk/reward logic + world expansion synchronized with capability.
- 《凡人修仙之仙界篇》: immediate threat + identity recovery + familiar-character return + action-state transitions + serial escalation.

Therefore YMGQ must not be repaired by simply increasing cliffhangers, shortening dialogue, or adding mystery. The correct question is what reader-desire engine belongs to THIS story.

## B. Prose surface: why these texts do not rely on constant 'writerly cleverness'

### B1. Unevenness is useful

Across the three sources, paragraphs and scenes are not uniformly maximized for dramatic effect. Ordinary description, practical thinking, errands, economy, travel, preparation, jokes and minor social friction coexist with high-pressure scenes.

This creates contrast and suppresses the sensation that every line has been engineered to perform one authorial function.

### B2. Not every paragraph closes its own meaning

The strongest source passages frequently allow observation, action or dialogue to stand without immediate narrator explanation. Meaning can remain for the reader to infer or can become clear only after a later beat.

Transfer lesson for YMGQ: repeated `event -> correct emotional interpretation -> author summary` creates excessive author-presence even when each interpretation is accurate.

### B3. Paragraph rhythm is varied, not all emphasis lines

Sampled paragraph medians:
- current 《诡秘之主》 file: ~31 Chinese characters;
- cleaner 《凡人修仙传》 sample: ~41;
- 《仙界篇》: ~36.

Very short paragraphs exist, but they are a minority rather than the default presentation surface. A manuscript dominated by one-sentence emphasis paragraphs can feel over-directed even without any individually bad sentence.

### B4. Explanation is not itself the enemy

《凡人修仙传》 uses substantial direct explanation and long practical paragraphs, yet can remain highly readable because the explanation usually attaches to a decision, resource, risk, rule or progression bottleneck the reader already cares about.

Transfer lesson: ban "explanation" is false. The failure is explanation that closes curiosity or exists mainly to demonstrate author clarity.

## C. Opening retention engines

### C1. 《诡秘之主》 early engine

The early chapters repeatedly use this pattern:

`embodied anomaly -> partial interpretation -> test/action -> answer -> larger anomaly or new role`

Important properties:
- questions are answered quickly enough to reward trust;
- answers often enlarge rather than close the model;
- the protagonist acts, tests and improvises instead of merely receiving exposition;
- family, money, work and humor remain present beside the supernatural mystery;
- by the first several chapters, a recurring social/identity engine has formed rather than relying on a single mystery.

Early chapter endings vary: body-horror discovery, concrete plan, emotional family beat, unexpected social event, identity performance, humor, knock at the door, dread-inducing reclassification. The engine is varied.

### C2. 《凡人修仙传》 early engine

The early attraction is much less mystery-dependent.

It begins with:
- understandable poverty and family conditions;
- a small, credible personal desire to leave the village and improve life;
- an opportunity whose value is immediately legible;
- entry tests, status uncertainty and incremental competence;
- eventually, a durable asymmetric resource/advantage.

The reader does not need a new riddle every chapter. The forward desire is: `Can this ordinary boy convert limited opportunity into a larger life, without being destroyed by the world around him?`

Progress itself becomes suspense.

### C3. 《仙界篇》 early engine

The sequel opens more aggressively:

`pursuit -> mysterious powerful man appears -> threat removed -> relationship bond -> new threat -> hostile helper/ritual -> identity recovery`

The reader receives frequent state changes. Early endings are often physical or relational pivots rather than narrator summaries.

## D. Long-form retention: different engines, same deeper principle

Cross-book common principle:

> The reader must repeatedly acquire a stronger future-facing desire, not merely more information.

That future desire can be:
- answer ownership;
- progression;
- survival;
- relationship anticipation;
- identity performance;
- resource conversion;
- fear of a consequence;
- expectation of a promised confrontation;
- desire to see a character act under a new constraint.

A chapter can be information-rich and still fail retention if the reader's desire state is unchanged.

## E. Character personhood

### E1. Characters are not continuously optimized for plot

Strong source characters worry about money, food, family, embarrassment, status, convenience, irritation, hobbies, habits and social image. These may later matter, but they need not be immediately monetized into the central plot.

This produces incidental life.

### E2. Private priorities distort attention

Natural character attention is selective. Characters notice what matters to them, not necessarily what most efficiently advances the author's mystery.

Transfer lesson for YMGQ: even after emotion was added, characters can still feel AI-generated if every thought perfectly diagnoses their own emotional conflict and every question targets the next plot fact.

### E3. Competence is a reward

《凡人修仙传》 especially demonstrates that reader attachment can come from watching a protagonist reason cautiously, preserve options, distrust seductive shortcuts and convert small resources into survival advantage.

Character competence itself is narrative payoff.

## F. Dialogue and anti-AI evidence

Dialogue ratios in stratified samples differ substantially:
- current 《诡秘之主》 file: ~34% of sampled paragraphs begin as dialogue;
- cleaner 《凡人修仙传》 sample: ~22%;
- 《仙界篇》: ~24%.

There is no universal dialogue-density target.

What matters more:
- speaker goal and status;
- what the speaker would avoid saying;
- humor and personality;
- socially inconvenient details;
- interruption and partial understanding;
- whether the listener's response is driven by private concerns rather than author information order.

The human-located YMGQ defect `short question -> exact short answer -> exact follow-up -> clean closure` is therefore better understood as an over-regularized author-serving cadence, not as a short-sentence problem.

## G. Information control

### G1. Strong mysteries create model change

The strongest mystery beats do not simply add another fact. They make the reader revise a previous explanation.

Useful pattern:

`working model -> new evidence -> model becomes insufficient -> new action becomes necessary`

This differs from:

`question -> answer -> next unrelated question`.

### G2. Answers must generate consequence

An answer can produce:
- a new risk;
- a new role;
- a new decision;
- a new enemy;
- a relationship reclassification;
- a new resource constraint;
- a larger question that was impossible to ask before.

If the answer only improves the reader's database, propulsion drops.

## H. Logic and reader trust

Across the three sources, readable long-form logic is less about explaining every detail and more about maintaining a stable causal contract:

- characters act from information available to them;
- power/resources have constraints;
- gains create new bottlenecks;
- preparation can later pay off;
- risk is not erased by convenient new facts;
- the protagonist's decisions are legible even when the outcome is uncertain.

This is especially strong in progression fiction: the reader continues because they trust the conversion chain between effort, resources, risk, knowledge and gain.

## I. Author technical-note evidence from the current 《诡秘之主》 source

The uploaded source contains a second-volume technical summary by the author. Abstracted conclusions relevant to this project:

1. The author explicitly values simple, precise language and emotional accumulation rather than constant ornamental prose.
2. He identifies an over-tight event rhythm as a weakness: continuous events without enough relaxed transition can fatigue readers.
3. He identifies recurring weekly structural cadence as a possible source of repetitive stiffness and proposes skipping low-value repetitions.
4. He deliberately suppresses minor unresolved branches near a major ending so they do not dilute the final emotional picture.
5. He treats minor ordinary people as necessary to make the world feel historically and emotionally real, not just as plot carriers.

These points strongly support the current YMGQ diagnosis: naturalness cannot be produced by making every line efficient; propulsion cannot be produced by keeping every scene maximally tense.

## J. Direct diagnosis transfer to 《一命过桥》

Current YMGQ has improved affect and interiority but still differs from the source strengths in several ways:

1. **Too much semantic closure.** The narrator often explains the exact emotional meaning of a beat immediately after it happens.
2. **Too much authorial symmetry.** Short paragraphs and dialogue beats are repeatedly shaped as clean dramatic units.
3. **Characters are too self-aware.** They frequently understand their own conflicting emotions more clearly than real people usually do.
4. **Too little incidental life.** Almost every object, memory, action and exchange exists to serve the case/mystery.
5. **Backward-facing information dominates.** Many active questions concern what happened years ago; answers reduce uncertainty without necessarily creating a stronger future desire.
6. **The current future is too predictable.** Preserve Gu Chengyue, reveal more of the old case, reach the bridge, obtain testimony: the route is legible before the reader reaches it.
7. **Reward diversity is low.** There is relatively little humor, relationship pleasure, competence reward, discovery pleasure, social surprise or ordinary-life attachment competing with the central grievance.

## K. First-pass cross-book production principles

Do NOT expose these as a generator checklist. They are coordinator-level craft evidence.

- Build one story-specific forward desire engine before optimizing sentence style.
- Make answers change what can happen next.
- Let character private priorities distort clean information delivery.
- Allow some details and interactions to exist as life, not clues.
- Vary chapter/scene closure mechanisms.
- Preserve quiet periods when they build attachment, contrast or future cost.
- Do not explain every emotional meaning immediately.
- Do not turn every paragraph into a designed emphasis beat.
- Make protagonist agency and decision quality part of the reward.
- Tie worldbuilding to current use, desire, risk or wonder rather than encyclopedic completeness.
