## A. 《诡秘之主》 partial-source second pass

### LOTM2-01 — uploaded global ordinal 1 — physical anomaly before explanation
The reader begins with a bodily/sensory problem the protagonist must inspect. World information is learned through attempts to understand an immediate impossibility.
**Propulsion mechanism:** curiosity is owned by the protagonist's survival/model problem rather than by narrator lore delivery.

### LOTM2-02 — uploaded global ordinal 6 — anomaly creates a recurring social role
An experiment does not merely answer a mystery; it forces the protagonist to improvise an identity and creates a repeatable meeting/exchange structure.
**Propulsion mechanism:** one answer opens a durable story machine: role maintenance, exchange, reputation and asymmetric information.

### LOTM2-03 — uploaded global ordinals 20–40 — ordinary economy and humor remain visible
Work, money, food, embarrassment and playful social interaction coexist with supernatural learning.
**Propulsion mechanism:** non-task life creates affection and contrast; explanation is easier to absorb when the scene also contains social pleasure and practical wants.

### LOTM2-04 — uploaded global ordinal 78 — reward type changes inside one chapter
After danger, relationship banter and affection provide relief; the chapter later reopens immediate uncertainty through a concrete physical reversal.
**Propulsion mechanism:** continuation is not one repeated cliffhanger cadence. Social pleasure and threat can alternate.

### LOTM2-05 — uploaded global ordinals 180–240 — information changes role or choice
Large knowledge payloads matter because participants have different information, goals and future uses for what they learn. The protagonist repeatedly converts knowledge into a hypothesis, transaction, disguise or decision.
**Propulsion mechanism:** explanation remains active when it changes what a character can now attempt.

### LOTM2-06 — uploaded global ordinal 420 — collaborative inference changes direction
Evidence is interpreted by multiple actors; a new hypothesis changes investigative action rather than merely producing a correct paragraph of explanation.
**Propulsion mechanism:** model delta must produce action delta.

### LOTM2-07 — uploaded global ordinal 480 — reward arrives with a new constraint
A relationship/resource payoff expands capability but also creates operational cost and future management questions.
**Propulsion mechanism:** reward opens the next lock instead of flattening pressure.

### LOTM2-08 — uploaded global ordinal 520 — agency survives scale expansion
The protagonist and allies choose an active hunt while a separate perspective widens the regional problem.
**Propulsion mechanism:** arc expansion preserves immediate goals and actor-owned intent.

## D. Cross-book mechanisms that survived second-pass contrast

### COMMON2-01 — Reader desire is a future-state question
The strongest recurring pattern is not “more mystery” but “what future state is now possible, threatened or newly costly?”

### COMMON2-02 — Explanation needs ownership
Explanation retains energy when someone needs it to choose, bargain, prepare, buy, hide, test, travel, fight, refuse or wait. Explanation becomes flat when it only updates the reader.

### COMMON2-03 — Agency creates trust
Readers can continue because they want to see how a protagonist will solve the next problem, even when there is no major mystery.

### COMMON2-04 — Intelligent threat changes the model
Threat is stronger when another actor owns an intention and can adapt, rather than functioning as anonymous arrows/weather.

### COMMON2-05 — Ordinary life is not decorative padding
Food, money, embarrassment, habits, minor bureaucracy, jokes and small social obligations can create attachment, contrast and future loss value.

### COMMON2-06 — Rewards expand or reprice options
A gain should alter what can be attempted, what must now be protected, or what larger problem becomes reachable.

### COMMON2-07 — Progression is possibility-space change
Power growth matters when a known limit, route, social status or task becomes newly possible.

### COMMON2-08 — Multi-agent scenes resist the clean Q&A machine
Different actors should possess different wants, knowledge and exit conditions. Their lines then arise from collision, not from orderly author information release.

### COMMON2-09 — Tonal and reward variance hides the authorial machine
Threat, affection, humor, competence, acquisition, discovery, embarrassment and moral choice can all carry continuation. Repeating only solemn reveal/tension makes design visible.

### COMMON2-10 — Page-turn propulsion and prose naturalness are separate
A passage may sound human yet remain flat; a passage may contain substantial exposition yet remain compelling. Sentence-level “AI smell” heuristics cannot substitute for desire-state analysis.

## E. Implication for 《一命过桥》

The current labelled manuscript has over-invested in retrospective questions: what happened years ago, who falsified what, who recognizes which object. Those answers mostly close history while leaving the next physical task predictable: keep 顾承岳 alive and move toward the bridge.

The next pilot therefore needs a different engine:

1. The bridge crossing itself must carry a future-facing consequence the protagonist personally owns.
2. 顾承岳 must become an agent with a goal of his own rather than protected cargo who dispenses backstory.
3. One piece of evidence/reveal must materially change 刘还山's decision about whether/how to complete the crossing now.
4. Loyalty must create a live cost: another person's decision to follow Liu should become something Liu may have to refuse, accept or pay for.
5. Ordinary behavior should establish a concrete possible “after the bridge” before pressure destroys or reprices it.
6. Suspense should ask “what will Liu choose after learning this?” rather than only “what happened before?”
7. The pilot should vary reward modes and avoid immediate narrator closure of every gesture or emotion.
8. No long continuation is authorized until the actual target reader reacts to the representative pilot.

This is a transfer hypothesis for one labelled manuscript. It does not prove a general writing architecture and does not advance the Phase320 accepted checkpoint.
