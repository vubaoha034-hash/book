## 1. Evidence base

This synthesis is derived from 90 new reader-experience cards:
- BOOK-01 《射雕英雄传》: 30 cards, 10 recoverable scene anchors
- BOOK-02 《诛仙》: 30 cards, 10 recoverable scene anchors
- BOOK-03 《盗墓笔记【壹】》: 30 cards, 10 recoverable scene anchors

Each book has 3 cards for each required dimension:
1. HOOK_AND_FIRST_PAGE_PROPULSION
2. PRESSURE_ESCALATION_AND_TENSION
3. MYSTERY_VS_CONFUSION_BOUNDARY
4. IMMEDIATE_MOTIVE_LEGIBILITY
5. INFORMATION_RELEASE_TIMING
6. CHARACTER_VOICE_DIFFERENTIATION
7. SCENE_ENTRY_EXIT_ENERGY
8. CONTINUOUS_SPAN_PACING
9. EMOTIONAL_ACCUMULATION_AND_PAYOFF
10. PROSE_SURFACE_ANTI_TEMPLATE_BEHAVIOR

The cards cover early, middle, and late material. They do not reproduce long source passages. They preserve scene identity, local objective, visible evidence, withheld information, beat-by-beat state change, reader reward, exit energy and failure boundary.

## 2. Core correction

The prior system over-weighted “what structural mechanism exists in the book.”
Phase372 adds the missing question:

> What does the reader know, want, fear, expect, receive, and newly question from beat to beat?

This is not a style imitation layer. It is a reader-state / scene-state transfer layer.

## 3. Cross-book reader-experience candidates

### RX-C01 — CLEAR_LOCAL_GOAL_OPEN_LARGER_QUESTION

**Core**
Useful suspense keeps the current action legible while withholding a larger cause, identity, consequence, or truth.

**Three-book support**
- 《射雕》: pursuit, identity revelation, sealed-order investigation and later political coercion all preserve clear current objectives while larger intent remains open.
- 《诛仙》: survivor investigation, 滴血洞, ten-year resurrection search and 草庙回返 keep “what the character needs now” clear even when the larger truth is unknown.
- 《盗墓》: water-cave rules, map contradictions, 玉俑 and 蛇眉铜鱼 provide a concrete local problem before expanding the larger mystery.

**Failure boundary**
If the reader cannot tell why the character is currently moving, refusing, hiding, attacking, checking, or protecting, the unknown is not automatically suspense; it is likely confusion.

**Status**
SOURCE-DERIVED_CANDIDATE / NOT_TRANSFER_PROVEN

### RX-C02 — STATE_CHANGE_DENSITY

**Core**
Reader propulsion correlates with repeated meaningful state changes: threat position, available option, relationship, evidence, leverage, goal, cost, or interpretation changes every few beats.

**Three-book support**
- 《射雕》追兵入村, 身世揭露, 锦囊密令 all repeatedly change the situation rather than merely restating danger.
- 《诛仙》陆雪琪首战 and 碧瑶痴情咒 payoff move through distinct states rather than one continuous mood.
- 《盗墓》血尸 opening and 七星疑棺 move abnormality → decision → consequence → new abnormality with little dead repetition.

**Failure boundary**
Fast sentences or frequent action do not count if the underlying state does not change.

**Status**
SOURCE-DERIVED_CANDIDATE / NOT_TRANSFER_PROVEN

### RX-C03 — EVIDENCE_BEFORE_HIGH_VALUE_EXPLANATION

**Core**
When explanation matters, concrete evidence, test, object, reaction, failed action or changed environment normally earns the explanation first.

**Three-book support**
- 《射雕》杨家枪法, 丐帮死讯 credibility and 锦囊 opening all make knowledge depend on observable evidence.
- 《诛仙》陆雪琪 power is proven by the bout; the 痴情咒 is first encountered as an object/choice before later meaning expands.
- 《盗墓》玉俑, 七星疑棺, 蛇眉铜鱼 and沉船葬 hypotheses are released after material findings.

**Failure boundary**
Exposition that benefits only the reader and does not alter the character's current model or choice is high-risk.

**Status**
SOURCE-DERIVED_CANDIDATE / NOT_TRANSFER_PROVEN

### RX-C04 — PRESSURE_BY_OPTION_LOSS_AND_COST

**Core**
Tension strengthens when choices become fewer, more expensive, less reliable, or more mutually incompatible.

**Three-book support**
- 《射雕》郭靖 late moral conflict makes each valid obligation incompatible with another.
- 《诛仙》痴情咒 payoff emerges only after normal solutions collapse; long-term rescue hope is constrained by real uncertainty.
- 《盗墓》海底墓 escape becomes tense as routes, equipment and expert certainty fail one by one.

**Failure boundary**
Repeated statements that “danger is getting closer” without changing options or cost create rhetorical tension, not dramatic tension.

**Status**
SOURCE-DERIVED_CANDIDATE / NOT_TRANSFER_PROVEN

### RX-C05 — MEMORY_ASSET_PAYOFF_WITH_CHANGED_FUNCTION

**Core**
A remembered object, place, rule, promise, relationship or phrase gains emotional force when it returns with a new causal function.

**Three-book support**
- 《射雕》old promises, family identity, sealed obligations and physical traces keep changing present choices.
- 《诛仙》痴情咒, 草庙村 and long relationship debts return with radically changed function.
- 《盗墓》蛇眉铜鱼 and prior explorers' maps convert remembered artifacts into current operational evidence.

**Failure boundary**
A callback that only says “remember this?” without changing current choice, cost, understanding or emotion is not sufficient.

**Status**
SOURCE-DERIVED_CANDIDATE / NOT_TRANSFER_PROVEN

### RX-C06 — VOICE_IS_DECISION_STYLE

**Core**
Character voice is more stable when differentiated by what the character notices, values, jokes about, distrusts, withholds, tests, or chooses under pressure—not by decorative catchphrases.

**Three-book support**
- 《射雕》丘处机, 柯镇恶, 朱聪, 李萍, 黄蓉 and郭靖 repeatedly differ in reasoning and decision method.
- 《诛仙》张小凡, 碧瑶, 鬼厉, 大巫师 and大竹峰众人 differ in value focus and speech timing.
- 《盗墓》潘子 favors action, 三叔 favors experienced inspection, 吴邪 hypothesis-building, 胖子 irreverent pragmatism, 闷油瓶 minimal evidence-backed authority.

**Failure boundary**
If all characters can exchange dialogue lines without changing scene logic, voice differentiation has failed even if vocabulary differs.

**Status**
SOURCE-DERIVED_CANDIDATE / NOT_TRANSFER_PROVEN

### RX-C07 — SCENE_EXIT_CREATES_NEXT_ACTION

**Core**
A strong scene exit leaves a changed actionable state: someone must now chase, verify, choose, hide, rescue, investigate, escape, confess or pay a cost.

**Three-book support**
- 《射雕》赌约, 锦囊 reveal and rescue traces create concrete next tasks.
- 《诛仙》会武 qualification, 痴情咒 discovery/payoff and大巫师 uncertainty change the next action.
- 《盗墓》missing companions, dashed-line room, 玉俑 identification and escape engineering all end by forcing a next move.

**Failure boundary**
“Danger remains” or “there are still many mysteries” without an altered next action is weak exit energy.

**Status**
SOURCE-DERIVED_CANDIDATE / NOT_TRANSFER_PROVEN

### RX-C08 — SURFACE_RHYTHM_MUST_FOLLOW_SCENE_FUNCTION

**Core**
Natural prose surface varies with scene function: dialogue, silence, physical action, object inspection, humor, compressed summary, sensory interruption, dense explanation and sparse aftermath should not share one repeated beat pattern.

**Three-book support**
- 《射雕》moves between banter, negotiation, martial evidence, ritual grief and reflective aftermath.
- 《诛仙》moves between domestic comedy, underdog embarrassment, arena spectacle, sacrifice and quiet post-climax drift.
- 《盗墓》mixes first-person jokes, technical inference, folk rules, abrupt horror and team problem-solving.

**Failure boundary**
Mechanical alternation such as “one line dialogue → one facial action → one explanation” creates AI-like regularity even when every sentence is locally grammatical.

**Status**
SOURCE-DERIVED_CANDIDATE / NOT_TRANSFER_PROVEN

## 4. Important negative findings

Phase372 does **not** support the claim that:
- shorter sentences automatically improve pacing;
- every dialogue line needs an action beat;
- more mysteries produce more suspense;
- withholding motive is desirable;
- constant danger is required for tension;
- every scene must end on a cliffhanger;
- every recurring object should become a major payoff;
- these three books share one prose style.

The shared evidence is more limited and more useful:
- local clarity can coexist with large unknowns;
- state change matters more than sentence speed;
- pressure needs changing options/cost;
- explanation needs an in-scene reason;
- voice comes from cognition and choice;
- surface rhythm should adapt to scene function;
- payoff earns strength from prior reader memory.

However, because Phase372 candidates were synthesized after those YMGQ observations, those observations are calibration evidence, not an independent transfer test.
